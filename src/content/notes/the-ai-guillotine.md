The AI Guillotine: cut the ballast before you automate the business.

Do you really know which business processes should be automated?

Before implementing AI, companies usually ask: "Which process can we hand over to AI?"

But the first question should be different: "Does this business process still need to exist at all?"

There is no point in digitizing ballast. You need to get rid of it.

I call this the **AI Guillotine**. It is part of business process diagnostics before AI implementation.

First, we cut off everything unnecessary: duplicated, outdated, excessive, and inefficient processes.

Only after that do we look at what can be handed over to AI, what should remain with employees, and what should be rebuilt as "employee + AI."

Why does this matter?

Because AI does not eliminate excessive processes. It simply makes them faster.

The AI Guillotine is not something I invented out of thin air.

I once took part in similar work for a municipality. As a result, we managed to reduce the total delivery time across 105 municipal services by 513 days, and eliminate 6 services completely.

Back then, it had nothing to do with AI. But now I see that the same logic works perfectly for preparing companies for AI implementation.

The idea is simple:

- Do not automate ballast.
- First, cut off what is unnecessary.
- Then give AI what can genuinely work faster, cheaper, and better.

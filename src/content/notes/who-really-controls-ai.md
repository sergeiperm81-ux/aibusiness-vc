Who really controls AI?

Usually, the answer is data centers, chips, energy, and the biggest models. That is a conversation for governments and corporations.

But it leaves out the most important player: you.

All of that AI is built for one reason: to serve ordinary people. So you can control its quality too. Right where you meet it, at the counter.

You do not need a technical background. You do not need to look inside the black box. You judge an AI service the way you judge any service: Was it clear? Was it polite? Did it solve my problem on the first try? Could I reach a human? Could I appeal?

For years, I assessed the quality of public services as both a researcher and a practitioner: nationwide monitoring, independent reviews, mystery shopping. I have now adapted that method to services delivered by AI. The good news: it works almost unchanged.

I put it all into a short, free guide: [Consumer Control of AI](/library/consumer-control-of-ai).

Inside:

- why an ordinary person can control AI
- the four steps of consumer control
- your existing rights as levers (EU AI Act Art. 50, 85, 86; GDPR Art. 22)
- a ready-to-use Assessment Card you can apply to any AI service today

Good feedback makes the AI better, customers happier, and the business stronger. Everyone wins.
